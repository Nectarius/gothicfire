We want to introduce a mini-game mode for combat. When a user attacks another player or the computer, this mini-game mode is triggered. A map of the location appears, featuring a grid for movement. Depending on the troops recruited, a player may have between 1 and 5 units on the board:
1.  **Hero:**
*   *Warrior Hero:* Receives a bonus to defense and attack; can move twice and then attack. 
*   *Archer Hero:* Can move three times and attack targets up to two squares away (horizontally or vertically). 
*   *Mage Hero:* Can move twice and attack targets up to three squares away (in any direction).

3.  **Heavy Infantry:** Can move twice and attack adjacent squares (in any direction); possesses a higher defense bonus than an archer.
4.  **Light Infantry:** (Available if recruited) Can move three times and attack adjacent squares (in any direction).
5.  **Mage:** Can move twice and attack targets up to two squares away (in any direction).
