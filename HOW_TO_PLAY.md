# How to Play Gothic Fire

Welcome to **Gothic Fire**! This is a turn-based multiplayer tactical strategy game where you recruit armies, manage resources, and engage in tactical combat across a dynamic territory map.

## The Goal
The objective of Gothic Fire is to lead your team (Red, Blue, or Yellow) to victory by conquering territories, building up your economy, and destroying rival armies. The game ends when one team achieves dominance or when the maximum number of turns (80) is reached.

## The Map and Territories
The map is divided into multiple **Sectors**. Each sector can be upgraded and provides resources:
- **Food**: Essential for sustaining your armies.
- **Gold**: The primary currency used to recruit new units and purchase upgrades.
- **Cultivation**: Determines the resource output (Food and Gold) of the territory.
- **Protection**: Provides defensive bonuses during combat. High protection makes a territory harder to conquer.

## Your Character (Commander)
You play as a Commander leading your armies. Your character has four main stats that influence combat effectiveness:
- **Warlord**: Improves the effectiveness of Light and Heavy Infantry.
- **Archon**: Boosts the power of Mages in your army.
- **Vanguard**: Provides a strong base combat bonus, particularly effective when commanding Archers or leading small elite forces (under 10 units).
- **Intellect**: Grants a strategic combat bonus whenever your army includes Mages.

## Army Units
There are four types of units you can recruit into your army, each with a different base combat power:
1. **Light Infantry** (Power: 1.0) - Cheap, basic melee fighters.
2. **Archers** (Power: 1.5) - Ranged attackers that synergize well with Vanguard commanders.
3. **Heavy Infantry** (Power: 2.0) - Resilient melee fighters.
4. **Mages** (Power: 3.0) - The most powerful units, devastating when commanded by an Archon.

*Note: You can also acquire **Siege Weapons**, which are highly effective at negating the Protection bonus of enemy territories during attacks (requires 20+ territory protection to take effect).*

## Combat Mechanics
When two armies clash, combat is resolved based on a combination of army strength, commander stats, territory protection, and a bit of luck (a 20-sided dice roll for each side).

### Overwhelming Force
If one army is more than **10 times larger** than the opponent's, it wins automatically and instantly, suffering minimal casualties (maximum 5%), while the losing army is completely wiped out.

### Battle Strategies
Before attacking, a commander can choose a specific battle strategy. If you meet the stat and army size requirements, you gain a significant combat bonus:
- **Arcane Phalanx**: Requires Archon (6+) and an army larger than 5.
- **Hammer and Spell**: Requires Warlord (6+) and an army larger than 3.
- **Spell Infused Volley**: Requires Vanguard (6+) and an army larger than 5.

### Casualties and Defeat
- The **loser** of a battle loses their entire army and their character dies.
- The **winner** takes casualties based on how close the battle was. A hard-fought battle will result in higher losses than an easy victory.

## Random Events
Keep an eye out for **Nature Events** that can randomly occur during the game, heavily impacting the map:
- **Abundant Harvest / Volunteers**: Positive events that boost your resources or army.
- **Hurricane / Flood**: Negative events that can devastate territories.

## Turn Summary
1. **Gather Resources**: Collect Food and Gold from your controlled territories.
2. **Recruit & Upgrade**: Spend Gold to recruit new units (Infantry, Archers, Mages) or upgrade your territories' Cultivation and Protection.
3. **Move & Attack**: Move your armies across the map, claim neutral sectors, or attack enemy territories to expand your empire.
4. **End Turn**: Once all players have acted or time runs out, the next turn begins.

Good luck, Commander! The fires of war await.
